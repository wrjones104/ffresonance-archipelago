# Final Fantasy Resonance

## What does randomization do to this game?

Chests that hold consumables, equipment or gil become Archipelago locations. Their contents go into the multiworld item pool, and the chests themselves give 1 gil. The real reward arrives from the server, from whichever world it was placed in.

Optionally (experimental), the shining spots ("shinies") on the world map are locations too. Locations are named after the game's own maps, for example "Village of Kol Chest 1".

Also experimental, and on by default: party members (Fina, Lid, Nichol, Archwitch Fina, Jake, Sakura), all 26 visions and the key items go into the item pool, one of each, in place of filler. There is no logic for them yet, and the story still hands out its own copies, so for now an item from Archipelago is an early copy. Chests and shinies that hold key items or visions keep them, so the story plays as normal.

## What is the goal?

Reaching the story checkpoint set by the **Goal Story Checkpoint** option. The game counts story progress from 1020 to 8050, and the thousands digit is the chapter.

## What does another world's item look like in Final Fantasy Resonance?

The chest gives 1 gil, and an on-screen message names the item and the player it was sent to.

## What happens when the player receives an item?

The mod puts it in the inventory and shows "Received ..." on screen. A party member joins the active party when there is room, and the roster otherwise. A vision received before the story gives the first ones (in the Town of Mitra) waits until then.
