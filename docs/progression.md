# Game progression reference

A chapter-by-chapter overview of party members, vehicles, access gates, Visions (26) and Espers (9), for planning Archipelago logic.

**Source:** the project owner's notes. Not yet checked against game data. Where the game's own data says something different, the data wins. Notes on that are marked **[data]**.

## Prologue: Battlefield

- **Playable:** a temporary Lv. 55 Archwitch Fina ("Dark Fina" early in the story).
- **Location:** Battlefield.
- **Progression:** a linear tutorial with no items or checks. It ends with a scripted fight against an Iron Giant, where you summon Bahamut on turn 3.

## Chapter 1: Tailing the Truth

- **Lands:** Kingdom of Grandshelt, Kolobos Island, Grandport.
- **Party:** Rain (Sentinel) and Lasswell (Breaker/Stagger) from the start. Fina (Medic/Summoner) joins on leaving Grandshelt Castle after the Earth Shrine events.
- **Flow:**
  - Grandshelt Castle and Earth Shrine: the tutorial dungeon. Boss: Belmodar.
  - Town of Mitra: story events give the first two Visions (Tronn and Leah). Return to Grandshelt Castle during an imperial attack (bosses: Hell's Rider, then a scripted loss to Umbra).
  - **Village of Kol (vehicle unlock):** rest at the inn to get a Chocobo. It is the gate to Felicitas and the Kolobos region.
  - Felicitas and Kolobos Ruins: clear the ruins (boss: The Hunger) to open the way to the port.
  - Grandport and Siren's Tower: talk to the sailor at Grandport pier to open Siren's Tower. Beat Siren at the top to end the chapter, then take a ship to Dirnado.
- **Visions:**
  - Tronn and Leah (Mitra story).
  - Warrior of Light (Sanctum in Grandshelt/Grandport).
  - Terra (Sanctum in SE Grandshelt/Kolobos).
  - Cloud (Sanctum in NW Grandshelt Desert).
  - Clive's Sanctum is in Grandshelt but needs the airship (ch. 5).
- **Espers:** Siren (automatic on beating her at the top of Siren's Tower).
- **Optional:** Cavern of Swords, Marauder Cave (Marauder), Gilgamesh, Antlion.

## Chapter 2: Eternal Blue

- **Lands:** Dirnado, the Continent of Wind.
- **Party:** Lid (Machinist/Support) joins after you talk to her at the Dilmagia Shipyard.
- **Flow:**
  - Dilmagia, the Machinopolis: meet Lid. Her airship is grounded and needs a crystal from the Wind Shrine and parts from the Dwarven Forge.
  - Mt. Thunderclap: overland mountain dungeon, with an optional 1,000-gil shortcut offered by Lid. Boss: Liquid Thunder.
  - Dwarven Forge: talk to Borin about airship parts and clear the forge monster (boss: Mom Bomb). Borin is then kidnapped to Mobliz Shipyard.
  - **Mobliz Shipyard (temporary point of no return):** an elevator/lever puzzle (5 + 8 + 3 = 16: pull lever 16), then a 15-minute timed escape ending on a cargo train (boss: Adrastea). The train crashes near the Dwarven Forge.
  - Wind Shrine: opens after reporting back to the Dwarven Forge. Beat Aether (Veritas of the Heavens) and Guardian.
  - Crash: you board Lid's repaired airship at the end of the chapter, and it crash-lands on Olderion.
- **Visions:**
  - Zidane (Sanctum near Mt. Thunderclap).
  - Noctis (Sanctum on the left fork toward Mobliz Shipyard).
  - Onion Knight (Sanctum through the NW entrance of Mt. Thunderclap near the Wind Shrine, or by airship later).
- **Espers:** Ramuh (hidden path in Mt. Thunderclap near the Dwarven Forge; answer "It's terrifying" to challenge him).
- **Side quests and optional:**
  - "Arise, Mechabo!": talk to the blacksmith Verun at the Dwarven Forge after Mobliz Shipyard and bring Dynamo Stones from Ipoohs. This repairs Mechabo and unlocks a chapter 3 follow-up that teaches Lid Mecha Fat Chocobo.
  - Optional boss: Shoggoth, in Shoggoth Cave.

## Chapter 3: Wardens of the Aquapolis

- **Lands:** Olderion Federation.
- **Party:** Nichol (Strategist; water and ice mage) joins during the opening events in Olderion and the Cave of Waters. Archwitch Fina is briefly playable again during a story sequence, with Fina's Mastery levels.
- **Flow:**
  - Olderion (Aquapolis) and Cave of Waters: a barrier blocks the Water Shrine. Find Nichol by the NW canal and follow him to the Cave of Waters. Use water-level switches to move boats and rescue Luka (boss: Wave Lopros).
  - **Old Town, Olderion (temporary point of no return):** after you try to purify three canal spots with Luka, monsters attack Old Town. Clear it and help Elle beat the Water Dragon.
  - Lake Dorr: through the north exit of the Cave of Waters. Fight Leviathan by destroying light orbs so Luka's song can weaken it. This does not unlock Leviathan as an Esper yet.
  - Waterfall Way and Altar of the Water Maiden: rest at the Olderion inn to trigger the floods. Go west through Waterfall Way for the First Water Maiden's power.
  - Amore, Warden's Cape and Water Shrine:
    - Travel through Amore to Warden's Cape.
    - Nichol and Elle reveal the hidden route and drop the Water Shrine's barrier.
    - Inside, solve the water-level and floating-crate puzzles, plus the Left-Right-Left switch puzzle for the optional R'lyeh Text.
    - Beat Alluvion (Veritas of the Waters).
- **Visions:**
  - Firion (Sanctum in the forest south of Olderion).
  - Squall (Sanctum on the east coast road).
  - Tidus (Sanctum far west, past the waterfalls).
- **Espers:** Carbuncle. Read the stele at the back of Ruby Cave first. That spawns 3 Ruby Fragments on the Olderion overworld: SE between two ponds, on the centre-east coast, and west of the waterfalls. Return all 3 to the stele; there is no boss fight. **[data]** Ruby Fragment is item 20030.
- **Optional:** Rukh Cave (Rukh).

## Chapter 4: Banner Oath

- **Lands:** Zoldaad Empire territory.
- **Party:** Jake (rebel leader; gunner) meets the party on the train and joins at the Rebel Hideout beneath Zoldaad.
- **Flow:**
  - Mozul Station and the train escape: you land in eastern Zoldaad. The Imperial Capital is closed until you board the train at Mozul Station. Jake frames the party with a rebel rose, which forces a combat escape on the train.
  - Imperial Capital and Underground Tunnels: use the streetcars to visit the marked districts, then follow Jake through the tunnels to the Rebel Hideout. The Grandport Colosseum opens at the start of chapter 4.
  - Magitech Weapons Factory:
    - Needs the Factory ID Card, which Jake's rebels already stole.
    - Lava bridge switches and rotating platforms.
    - An unwinnable fight against Ignis (Veritas of the Flame). Lasswell then awakens his Shiden blade, and you beat Ignis in the rematch.
  - Zoldaad Dungeon and Castle: infiltrate through a second branch of the tunnels. After a scripted capture, Amelia frees you. Beat the guard for the Master Key / Dungeon Key (opens Prince Shera's cell), and talk to the captain upstairs for the Throne Room Key. **[data]** Dungeon Key 20019, Throne Room Key 20021.
  - **Throne Room and The Invincible (airship unlock):**
    - Find the secret behind the painting.
    - Beat Emperor Sosa and Lazarov (plus Evan), and rescue Prince Shera.
    - The airship, The Enterprise, is yours for good at the end of the chapter.
- **Visions:** Shantotto, Cecil and Y'shtola (Sanctums across Zoldaad).
- **Espers:** Titan, reachable once the Colosseum opens. Win Fledgling Battle 5 for the Gaia Hammer, then break the sealing stone in Grandshelt Desert east of Siren's Tower.
- **Optional:** Gogmagog.

## Chapter 5: Scars of Time

- **Lands:** Mysidia (the Uncharted Land) and Paladia (Fina's psyche).
- **Party:**
  - Sakura (Sage/Mage) joins early, at her house in Mysidia.
  - Archwitch Fina (Saboteur; dark mage, whips) joins for good at the end of chapter 5 / start of 6. She joins at Lv. 55 with all of regular Fina's Mastery levels.
- **Flow:**
  - **Finding Mysidia (radar gate):** board the Enterprise outside Zoldaad. Fly over the empty ocean south and a little east of Pharm, and use the Radar Pulse with Lid's hints to reveal Mysidia.
  - Mysidia (Magiapolis), Sunioo and the Nameless Ruins: visit Sakura's house and the potion factory, then Sunioo, then clear the Nameless Ruins. The statue puzzle for Tyr's Armor is: light Broken Sword, then Withered Horse, then Darkness Sealed.
  - Arcane Library: defeat 5 Monster Tomes (bosses include Phantom Train True Form, Iron Giant, Typhon and Byblos) to open the forbidden wing.
  - Fina's Psyche and the Tower of Mysidia: enter Fina's psyche from the library, then climb the tower. At the summit, re-enter her psyche and fight Archwitch Fina (Lv. 38 boss).
  - Paladia memory (Hessian Base, Umbra's Camp, Battlefield): in the psyche's Paladia of 700 years ago, visit the Hessian base and Aldorian/Umbra's camp, talk to the Sworn Eight/Veritas, and fight Tellus (Veritas of the Earth) on the Battlefield.
- **Opened up by the airship in chapter 5:**
  - **Visions, Sanctums:** Clive (Grandshelt cliff), Bartz, Vaan and Lightning (all three in Mysidia).
  - **Visions, side quests:**
    - Aileen: "Forger of Wills" in Dilmagia. It also unlocks Adamantite ultimate-weapon crafting.
    - Camille: "The Call from Beyond", Zoldaad Imperial Residences.
    - Wilhelm: "O General! My General!", Zoldaad Castle Plaza.
    - Victoria: "Victoria Revivified", Mysidia.
    - Amelia: "Errand's End", on the Zoldaad notice board. It needs Jake's inn quest "Token Legacy" first.
  - **Vision, boss trial:** Sephiroth. Fly into Whirlpool Hollow, between Grandport and Grandshelt, and beat him at Lv. 45.
  - **Espers:**
    - Ifrit: fly into the volcano crater east of the Zoldaad Capital.
    - Shiva: land on the small ice-covered island SW of Mysidia, west of Jotunn Cave.
    - Leviathan: finish Nichol's side quest "Nichol's Duty" in Olderion, beat a Lv. 40 Sahagin SE of Olderion, and return to Lake Dorr. No boss fight.
  - **Optional bosses:** Slagworm, Salome.

## Chapter 6: Tranquility

- **Lands:** Continent of Gronoa.
- **Party:** all 8 members, with Archwitch Fina active from the start of the chapter.
- **Flow:**
  - Gaberada, the Undertown: dense miasma keeps the Enterprise off Gronoa. Land on the east edge and enter the underground town of Gaberada. The Bog of No Return, a miasma marsh, blocks the way west.
  - Research Center Ruins (keycards and Holy):
    - Go east of Gaberada for the White Jade Stone, so Fina can learn Holy.
    - Inside, the Level 1 Lab Key Card opens the way to the central teleporter.
    - A green portal maze leads to the Level 2 Lab Key Card.
    - The Level 3 Lab Key Card opens the basement lab.
    - **[data]** Key cards 20009–20011.
  - Clearing the Bog of No Return: return the White Jade Stone to the Gaberada elder. Fina learns Holy and purifies the bog, opening western Gronoa.
  - Rugged Mountain Path and the Town of Duggle: cross the pass (Rain and Lasswell clash) and shelter from the miasma rain in Duggle. Sanctissimus Shrine is east of Duggle, but Sakura stops you entering until the Gronoa Shrine is cleared.
  - Gronoa Shrine: beat Elfdrit (Elfried). You learn that Gelidus has moved the Dark Crystal.
  - Sanctissimus Shrine: read the inscriptions and clear the boss gauntlet: Archaoaevis, Dad Bomb, Test Rider, Yakhanati, two Arch Gargoyles, Lux, Gelidus, and a duel with Lasswell.
- **Espers:** Odin.
  - Once the bog is cleared, go east to the Bladerain Stele cave to start the "Iron Splitter" side quest.
  - Beat Odin's Shadow on the western precipice.
  - Take the sword to the Nameless Ruins in Mysidia.
  - **[data]** Broken Sword Hilt 20031 and Blade 20032.
- **Optional:** Dark Fang, Humbaba.

## Chapter 7: Reckoning

- **Lands:** Kingdom of Pharm and the Wandering Lands.
- **Flow:**
  - Tornado gate and the Town of Sian: a tornado south of Pharm keeps the airship from landing near the battlefield. Fly into it to fight Aether again, then land and walk to Sian to talk to Ayaka.
  - Mt. Reizan and Lake Ryusei: the airship cannot get past the storm, so climb Mt. Reizan on foot. Fight Alluvion and Ignis on the way. The Resonance Stone radar upgrade is in the chest past the two short wooden bridges.
  - Forest Path and the Wandering Lands: Lake Ryusei leads to the peak, where a floating path joins the Forest Path and the Wandering Lands. Beat Tellus on the path and confront Umbra (Veritas of the Dark) at the Dark Crystal.
  - Crystal Tower approach: the dimensional gate opens and pulls Lapis toward Palladia. Board the Enterprise for the Crystal Tower.
- **Visions:** Ayaka ("The Admirable Apprentice", from Ayaka in Sian) and Charlotte ("The Shield of the Weak", Grandport Harbor).
- **Espers:** Bahamut. It needs the other 8 first: Siren, Ramuh, Carbuncle, Titan, Ifrit, Shiva, Leviathan and Odin. With them, after the chapter 7 Aether fight, enter the airship cabin. Fina hears a voice; talk to Evan to fly to Bahamut's Lv. 50 trial.
- **Optional:** Wild Maneuverer, Malboro Menace.

## Chapter 8: Beyond Hope

- **Lands:** Crystal Tower.
- **Flow:**
  - **Outer wall (final point of no return):** a barrier pushes the Enterprise back from the summit. Land at the outer wall, where Sakura removes the barrier in a story sequence.
  - Ascent: climb the crystal platforms. Fight the event battle against Dante, then beat Behemoth (Alluvion and Lux give up their armour's last power so you can pass) and Sol.
  - Summit and final bosses:
    - Confront Chaotic Darkness (The Keen of Fury). After you deplete its HP once, it revives and absorbs normal attacks.
    - The world's prayers then unlock the Last Resonance: an ultimate attack that triggers your equipped Vision's Resonance and swaps the active party member for a reserve one.
    - Archwitch Fina sacrifices herself to save regular Fina, and the tower collapses.
    - Rain finishes the game in a 1v1 duel against Sol.

## Global collectibles

- **99 Tablet Shards**, in almost every town, dungeon and overworld segment. **[data]** Item 25004; seen as `BP_MapGimmick_FieldItem_Medal_C` spots.
- **Magic Pots (Citrine / Viridian Pots):** encounters in dungeons such as Siren's Tower, Mobliz Shipyard and the Wind Shrine. They ask for recovery items (Ether, Mist Ether and so on) and give large amounts of Mastery Points (MRP) in return. **[data]** FFRS_QoL tracks one as `BP_MapGimmick_BlockingBase_Child_MagicPot_C`.
- **26 Visions and Magicite Shards/Crysts:** Visions are the sub-job and ability system (Zidane teaches Steal, Leah teaches Banish), and awakening them needs Magicite. Shuffling Visions and awakening materials gives combat-progression checks. **[data]** Magicite Shard 25000, Magicite 25001, Magicite Cryst 25002. Visions are the 13000s (Amelia is 13017).
