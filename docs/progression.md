# Game progression reference

A chapter-by-chapter overview of party members, vehicles, access gates, Visions (26) and Espers (9), for planning Archipelago logic.

**Source:** project notes checked against game data. Notes referencing game dump findings are marked **[data]**.

## Gates at a glance

| Gate type | Item / trigger | Vanilla unlock point | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicle | Chocobo | Chapter 1 (Village of Kol) | Travel from Grandshelt into the Kolobos / Felicitas region. **[data]** Artifacts 30002 "Choboco Saddle" and 30011 "Chocobo Saddle-Bike". |
| Vehicle | Ferry / ship | Chapters 1, 3, 4 | Story transitions: Grandport to Dirnado (ch. 1->2), Olderion to Zoldaad (ch. 3->4). |
| Vehicle | Airship (The Enterprise) | End of chapter 4 / start of 5 | Full world-map flight. Needed for isolated Sanctums, volcanoes, ice islands and Whirlpool Hollow. **[data]** Key item 29520 "Magitek Airship Passkey". |
| Upgrade | Radar Pulse (base) | Chapter 5 (story) | Installed by Lid. Needed to scan the ocean SE of Pharm and reveal Mysidia. |
| Upgrade | Radar: Magi-tenna | Chapter 5+ (hidden NPC behind an inn) | Tier 1 world-map treasures (Treasures 1-5, including Adamantite). **[data]** Item 27006. |
| Upgrade | Radar: Resonance Stone | Chapter 7 (chest in Mt. Reizan) | Tier 2 world-map treasures. **[data]** Item 27007, in a chest kept vanilla (id >= 13000). |
| Upgrade | Radar: High-Power Generator | Late game (Seafaring Trader, 60,000 gil) | Tier 3 world-map treasures. **[data]** Item 27008. |
| Field skill | Gaia Hammer | Chapter 4/5+ (Colosseum Fledgling Battle 5) | Lid breaks the sealing stone in Grandshelt Desert (east of Siren's Tower) to reach Titan. **[data]** Colosseum proofs are items 29000-29003 (Fledgling's to Champion's Proof of Victory). |

## Prologue: Battlefield

- Playable: temporary Lv. 55 Archwitch Fina ("Dark Fina" early in the story).
- Location: Battlefield.
- Progression: linear tutorial with no items or checks. Concludes with a scripted fight against an Iron Giant, summoning Bahamut on turn 3.

## Chapter 1: Tailing the Truth

- Lands: Kingdom of Grandshelt, Kolobos Island, Grandport.
- Party: Rain (Sentinel) and Lasswell (Breaker/Stagger) from the start. Fina (Medic/Summoner) joins on leaving Grandshelt Castle after Earth Shrine events.
- Flow:
  - Grandshelt Castle and Earth Shrine: tutorial dungeon. Boss: Belmodar.
  - Town of Mitra: story events grant first two Visions (Tronn and Leah). Return to Grandshelt Castle during imperial attack (bosses: Hell's Rider, scripted loss to Umbra).
  - Village of Kol: rest at inn to unlock Chocobo (vehicle unlock), gating travel to Felicitas and Kolobos.
  - Felicitas and Kolobos Ruins: clear ruins (boss: The Hunger) to open route to port.
  - Grandport and Siren's Tower: speak to sailor at Grandport pier to unlock Siren's Tower. Defeat Siren to conclude chapter and board ship to Dirnado.
- Visions:
  - Tronn and Leah (Mitra story).
  - Warrior of Light (Sanctum in Grandshelt/Grandport).
  - Terra (Sanctum in SE Grandshelt/Kolobos).
  - Cloud (Sanctum in NW Grandshelt Desert).
  - Clive's Sanctum is in Grandshelt but requires the airship (ch. 5).
- Espers: Siren (automatic upon defeat at top of Siren's Tower).
- Optional: Cavern of Swords, Marauder Cave (Marauder), Gilgamesh, Antlion.

## Chapter 2: Eternal Blue

- Lands: Dirnado, the Continent of Wind.
- Party: Lid (Machinist/Support) joins after speaking with her at Dilmagia Shipyard.
- Flow:
  - Dilmagia, the Machinopolis: meet Lid. Her airship is grounded and requires a Wind Shrine crystal plus parts from Dwarven Forge.
  - Mt. Thunderclap: mountain dungeon with an optional 1,000-gil shortcut from Lid. Boss: Liquid Thunder.
  - Dwarven Forge: speak to Borin regarding airship parts and clear forge monster (boss: Mom Bomb). Borin is then kidnapped to Mobliz Shipyard.
  - Mobliz Shipyard (temporary point of no return): solve elevator puzzle (5 + 8 + 3 = 16: pull lever 16), followed by a 15-minute escape on cargo train (boss: Adrastea). Train crashes near Dwarven Forge.
  - Wind Shrine: opens after reporting back to Dwarven Forge. Defeat Aether (Veritas of the Heavens) and Guardian.
  - Crash: board Lid's repaired airship at chapter end, crashing on Olderion.
- Visions:
  - Zidane (Sanctum near Mt. Thunderclap).
  - Noctis (Sanctum on left fork toward Mobliz Shipyard).
  - Onion Knight (Sanctum through NW entrance of Mt. Thunderclap near Wind Shrine, or via airship later).
- Espers: Ramuh (hidden path in Mt. Thunderclap near Dwarven Forge; answer "It's terrifying" to challenge him).
- Side quests and optional:
  - "Arise, Mechabo!": speak to blacksmith Verun at Dwarven Forge after Mobliz Shipyard and deliver Dynamo Stones from Ipoohs. Repairs Mechabo and unlocks chapter 3 follow-up teaching Lid Mecha Fat Chocobo.
  - Optional boss: Shoggoth, in Shoggoth Cave.

## Chapter 3: Wardens of the Aquapolis

- Lands: Olderion Federation.
- Party: Nichol (Strategist; water and ice mage) joins during opening events in Olderion and Cave of Waters. Archwitch Fina is briefly playable again during story sequence.
- Flow:
  - Olderion (Aquapolis) and Cave of Waters: barrier blocks Water Shrine. Find Nichol by NW canal and follow to Cave of Waters. Use water switches to navigate boats and rescue Luka (boss: Wave Lopros).
  - Old Town, Olderion (temporary point of no return): monsters attack Old Town after attempting canal purification with Luka. Clear area and assist Elle against Water Dragon.
  - Lake Dorr: accessed via north exit of Cave of Waters. Fight Leviathan by destroying light orbs to enable Luka's song (does not unlock Leviathan as Esper yet).
  - Waterfall Way and Altar of the Water Maiden: rest at Olderion inn to trigger floods. Travel west through Waterfall Way for First Water Maiden's power.
  - Amore, Warden's Cape and Water Shrine: travel through Amore to Warden's Cape where Nichol and Elle dispel barrier. Solve water puzzles and defeat Alluvion (Veritas of the Waters).
- Visions:
  - Firion (Sanctum in forest south of Olderion).
  - Squall (Sanctum on east coast road).
  - Tidus (Sanctum far west, past waterfalls).
- Espers: Carbuncle. Read stele at rear of Ruby Cave to spawn 3 Ruby Fragments on Olderion overworld (between ponds SE, center-east coast, and west of waterfalls). Return all 3 to stele with no boss fight. **[data]** Ruby Fragment is item 20030.
- Optional: Rukh Cave (Rukh).

## Chapter 4: Banner Oath

- Lands: Zoldaad Empire territory.
- Party: Jake (rebel leader; gunner) encounters party on train and joins at Rebel Hideout beneath Zoldaad.
- Flow:
  - Mozul Station and train escape: arrive in eastern Zoldaad. Imperial Capital is gated until boarding train at Mozul Station. Jake frames party with rebel rose, forcing combat escape.
  - Imperial Capital and Underground Tunnels: use streetcars to reach marked districts, then follow Jake through tunnels to Rebel Hideout. Grandport Colosseum opens at chapter start.
  - Magitech Weapons Factory: requires Factory ID Card stolen by rebels. Navigate lava bridges and rotating platforms. Scripted unwinnable fight against Ignis (Veritas of the Flame); Lasswell awakens Shiden blade for rematch victory.
  - Zoldaad Dungeon and Castle: infiltrate via secondary tunnel branch. Amelia frees party after capture. Defeat guard for Master/Dungeon Key (opens Shera's cell), and speak to captain upstairs for Throne Room Key. **[data]** Dungeon Key 20019, Throne Room Key 20021.
  - Throne Room and The Invincible (airship unlock): solve painting puzzle, defeat Emperor Sosa and Lazarov, and rescue Prince Shera. The Enterprise is unlocked permanently at chapter end.
- Visions: Shantotto, Cecil and Y'shtola (Sanctums across Zoldaad).
- Espers: Titan, available once Colosseum opens. Win Fledgling Battle 5 for Gaia Hammer, then break sealing stone in Grandshelt Desert east of Siren's Tower.
- Optional: Gogmagog.

## Chapter 5: Scars of Time

- Lands: Mysidia (the Uncharted Land) and Paladia (Fina's psyche).
- Party: Sakura (Sage/Mage) joins early at her Mysidia residence. Archwitch Fina joins permanently at end of chapter 5 / start of 6 at Lv. 55 inheriting regular Fina's Mastery.
- Flow:
  - Finding Mysidia (radar gate): board Enterprise outside Zoldaad. Fly over ocean SE of Pharm and scan with Radar Pulse.
  - Mysidia (Magiapolis), Sunioo and Nameless Ruins: visit Sakura's house and potion factory, then clear Nameless Ruins. Statue puzzle order: Broken Sword, Withered Horse, Darkness Sealed.
  - Arcane Library: defeat 5 Monster Tomes (Phantom Train True Form, Iron Giant, Typhon, Byblos) to access forbidden wing.
  - Fina's Psyche and Tower of Mysidia: enter psyche from library, climb tower, and defeat Archwitch Fina (Lv. 38 boss) at summit.
  - Paladia memory: explore historical Hessian base and Umbra's camp, converse with Sworn Eight, and defeat Tellus (Veritas of the Earth) on Battlefield.
- Opened up by airship in chapter 5:
  - Visions, Sanctums: Clive (Grandshelt cliff), Bartz, Vaan and Lightning (all three in Mysidia).
  - Visions, side quests: Aileen ("Forger of Wills", unlocks Adamantite crafting), Camille ("The Call from Beyond"), Wilhelm ("O General! My General!"), Victoria ("Victoria Revivified"), Amelia ("Errand's End", requires Jake's "Token Legacy").
  - Vision, boss trial: Sephiroth. Fly into Whirlpool Hollow between Grandport and Grandshelt, and defeat him at Lv. 45.
  - Espers: Ifrit (crater east of Zoldaad Capital), Shiva (ice island SW of Mysidia), Leviathan (complete "Nichol's Duty" in Olderion, defeat Lv. 40 Sahagin SE, return to Lake Dorr).
  - Optional bosses: Slagworm, Salome.

## Chapter 6: Tranquility

- Lands: Continent of Gronoa.
- Party: all 8 members active, with Archwitch Fina available from chapter start.
- Flow:
  - Gaberada, the Undertown: miasma forces landing on east edge into underground town of Gaberada. Bog of No Return blocks western travel.
  - Research Center Ruins (keycards and Holy): retrieve White Jade Stone east of Gaberada; Level 1 Key Card opens teleporter, green maze holds Level 2 Card, Level 3 Card opens basement lab. **[data]** Key cards 20009-20011.
  - Clearing Bog of No Return: deliver White Jade Stone to Gaberada elder; Fina learns Holy to purify marsh and open western Gronoa.
  - Rugged Mountain Path and Town of Duggle: cross pass and shelter in Duggle. Sanctissimus Shrine is gated until Gronoa Shrine is cleared.
  - Gronoa Shrine: defeat Elfdrit (Elfried) to discover Dark Crystal movement.
  - Sanctissimus Shrine: clear boss gauntlet (Archaoaevis, Dad Bomb, Test Rider, Yakhanati, two Arch Gargoyles, Lux, Gelidus, and duel with Lasswell).
- Espers: Odin. Once bog clears, visit Bladerain Stele cave for "Iron Splitter" quest, defeat Odin's Shadow on western precipice, and deliver blade to Nameless Ruins. **[data]** Broken Sword Hilt 20031, Blade 20032.
- Optional: Dark Fang, Humbaba.

## Chapter 7: Reckoning

- Lands: Kingdom of Pharm and Wandering Lands.
- Flow:
  - Tornado gate and Town of Sian: enter southern tornado to fight Aether, then land and proceed on foot to Sian to consult Ayaka.
  - Mt. Reizan and Lake Ryusei: traverse Mt. Reizan on foot, defeating Alluvion and Ignis. Resonance Stone radar upgrade is in chest past two short bridges.
  - Forest Path and Wandering Lands: ascend to peak where floating path joins Wandering Lands. Defeat Tellus and confront Umbra at Dark Crystal.
  - Crystal Tower approach: dimensional gate pulls Lapis toward Paladia; board Enterprise for Crystal Tower.
- Visions: Ayaka ("The Admirable Apprentice", in Sian) and Charlotte ("The Shield of the Weak", Grandport Harbor).
- Espers: Bahamut. Requires other 8 Espers (Siren, Ramuh, Carbuncle, Titan, Ifrit, Shiva, Leviathan, Odin). Speak with Evan in airship cabin after chapter 7 Aether fight to access Lv. 50 trial.
- Optional: Wild Maneuverer, Malboro Menace.

## Chapter 8: Beyond Hope

- Lands: Crystal Tower.
- Flow:
  - Outer wall (final point of no return): barrier pushes Enterprise back; land at outer wall where Sakura dispels barrier.
  - Ascent: climb platforms, defeat Dante, Behemoth, and Sol.
  - Summit and final bosses: battle Chaotic Darkness (The Keen of Fury). Prayers trigger Last Resonance, swapping party members. Archwitch Fina sacrifices herself; Rain finishes in 1v1 duel against Sol.

## Global collectibles

- 99 Tablet Shards across towns, dungeons, and overworld. **[data]** Item 25004, `BP_MapGimmick_FieldItem_Medal_C`.
- Magic Pots (Citrine / Viridian Pots): dungeon encounters (Siren's Tower, Mobliz Shipyard, Wind Shrine) trading recovery items (Ether, Mist Ether) for Mastery Points (MRP). **[data]** `BP_MapGimmick_BlockingBase_Child_MagicPot_C`.
- 26 Visions and Magicite Shards/Crysts: sub-job ability system awakened via Magicite. **[data]** Magicite Shard 25000, Magicite 25001, Magicite Cryst 25002. Visions use 13000s (Amelia 13017).
