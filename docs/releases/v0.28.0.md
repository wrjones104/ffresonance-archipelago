# v0.28.0 (apworld 0.28.0, mod 0.59.0): public playtest

This is the first public playtest build of the Final Fantasy Resonance randomizer for [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg/). It fixes what the v0.27.0 alpha testers found: the airship radar, places behind bosses that were in logic too early, and bosses that small parties could not beat. Expect bugs and unfinished pieces. If you try a seed, please report how it went using the feedback template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md), which also covers what to look out for.

## Downloads

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `FFRArchipelago.zip` | UE4SS Lua game mod (v0.59.0, build `1c03b1ed`) |
| `ff_resonance.apworld` | Archipelago world and Final Fantasy Resonance Client (v0.28.0, build `6df97dd3`) |
| `Final Fantasy Resonance.yaml` | Template player YAML with default options |

Make sure the mod and the apworld come from the same release. The mod writes its version and build to `UE4SS.log` on load (`version 0.59.0 (build 1c03b1ed)`), and the client prints its own when it starts. The client warns you if a seed was generated with a different apworld build.

## Changes since v0.27.0

### Fixes

- **Airship radar.** The radar now works. Each Progressive Radar opens its whole set of treasures at once (1 to 5, then 6 to 9, then 10 to 13), so you can dig them in any order.
- **Radar parts.** Mt. Reizan's chest no longer gives the vanilla Resonance Stone. With radar treasures on, it is a location like any other chest.
- **Nameless Ruins.** The stairs past Tellus lead to the upper floor again, so Nameless Ruins Chest 4 can be reached.
- **Boss zones.** Tellus, Lux, Elfdrit and Ignis start their fights the first time you walk into their zone. Before, the zone often did nothing until you had picked up a shiny or opened a chest on the same map.
- **Boss zone sizes.** Elfdrit's and the Forest Path Tellus's zones are larger in their own shape. The alpha's enlargement had made them smaller and easy to walk past.
- **Tower of Mysidia.** Walking into Tellus's zone from the side no longer leaves your character stuck walking into the stairs.
- **Tellus in the Forest Path.** The fight is now the "Forest Path: Tellus" location. It used to be named after the Wandering Lands.
- **Magicite shinies.** The two world map shinies that held a Magicite Shard (Dirnado and Pharm) are now shuffled locations.
- **Universal Tracker.** Oblivion Palace's chests come into logic as you receive Tablet Shards. Before, the tracker counted only some of the shards.
- **Client.** The client finds the mod in the `Mods` folder, and in any Steam library folder.

### Logic

- **Places behind bosses** wait for that boss in logic. These include Tower of Mysidia Chest 3 (Tellus), Earth Shrine Chests 4 to 6 (Belmodar), and Gronoa's south-west plateau with Duggle, Gronoa Shrine and Sanctissimus Shrine (Lux). They also include Pharm's plateau with Lake Ryusei, the Forest Path and the Wandering Lands (Ignis), and, on foot, Dragon's Maw (Intangir or the Airship).
- **Magicite is progression.** Vision awakening counts the Magicite you have received: two Magicite Shards for the first level, two Magicite for the second and two Magicite Crysts for the third. Bosses that need a Vision's awakened skills stay out of logic until then. In the alpha, opened continents stood in for Magicite.
- **Bosses that act twice a turn** (Lux, Tellus, Elfdrit, Gelidus, Behemoth and others) need one more party member than their tier asks for, up to five.
- **Tablet Shards** are all progression items.

### Gameplay

- **Normal strength is softer.** On Normal, bosses now scale to 105 to 110% of your party's level (was 110 to 115%), with 105 to 110% power (was 115 to 120%). Casual and Expert are unchanged.

### Upgrading from v0.27.0

Replace the `FFRArchipelago` folder and the apworld, then generate a new seed. Logic fixes only apply to seeds generated with this apworld. The new mod also works on a v0.27.0 seed, but on an old seed the two Magicite Shard shinies give nothing.

## What is in this build

### Open-world start

Pick **New Game (Archipelago)** on the title screen to skip the story and drop onto your starting continent with your starting party, in the airship or on foot. Normal **New Game** and your existing vanilla saves stay untouched; the mod does nothing while a vanilla save is loaded.

### Locations and items

A default seed has 469 locations:

| Kind | Count |
|---|---|
| Treasure chests | 206 |
| World map and dungeon shinies | 94 |
| Tablet Shard shinies | 99 |
| Sanctum of Light crystals | 16 |
| Boss fights (21 story bosses, 15 cave masters, 4 others) | 40 |
| Airship radar treasures, and the chest that held a radar part | 14 |

Story bosses hold Crystal Fragments for the `final_boss` goal. Aside from Sephiroth, the remaining bosses are priority locations by default.

Along with regular equipment, consumables, gil, and Magicite, the item pool can contain:
- 9 continent Unlocks
- 8 party members (all except your starting party), joining at your party's average level
- 26 visions
- 29 key items (keys, key cards, quest items, Colosseum proofs)
- The Airship and Chocobo, if you do not start with them
- 3 Progressive Radars for airship radar treasures
- 99 Tablet Shards

Continents stay sealed until you receive their Unlock item, which blocks airship landings, both bridges, and ferry routes to them. Without the Airship, you travel on foot and by ferry, so Mysidia, Gronoa, Pharm, and the Crystal Tower are unreachable until you get it.

### Goals and options

Two goals are supported:
- `final_boss` (default): the top of the Crystal Tower stays sealed until you collect 6 party members, 12 visions, and 14 Crystal Fragments, then win the final battle.
- `tablet_shards`: receive 50 Tablet Shards.

YAML options cover the goal and its required counts, starting continent, starting party (1 to 4 of the 8 members by name), starting with the Airship or Chocobo, radar treasures on or off, priority bosses, filler-only regions, and whether party members, visions, and key items are shuffled. The template YAML documents each option.

### Logic and in-game features

Locations enter logic once you can reach them, and boss fights also check whether your party can win. Bosses require a party size and continent count based on their tier, a healer from the middle tiers up, and for many bosses a way to hit their weakness or survive their worst attack. Character skills count from the level they are learned, vision skills count by their awakening level from the Magicite you have, and gear counts only when a reachable shop sells it. Places behind a boss wait for that boss. [Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases) shows what is in logic while you play.

In game:
- Enemies and bosses scale to your party level, with bosses scaling a bit higher.
- At the bottom of **Options > Gameplay**, you can change scaling strength (Casual, Normal, Expert), EXP, gil, and MRP rates, random encounters, and the encounter rate at any time.
- A feed at the bottom right shows items as they arrive, with progress counts on goal items (such as `Crystal Fragment (3/14)`).
- Chests holding another player's item tell you whose it is and what it is.
- The client links the save created by **New Game (Archipelago)** to your slot. Use `/bind` to link another save, or `/resync` to resend any missed checks.

## Requirements and installation

You need *Final Fantasy Resonance* on Steam (Windows), a UE4SS build for Unreal Engine 5.6 (`v3.0.1 Beta` or newer), and Archipelago 0.6.7 or newer. See the [README](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago#installation) for full details, or follow the quick steps below:

1. Install UE4SS into `FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE\FFRS\Binaries\Win64\`.
2. Extract `FFRArchipelago.zip` into `...\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\` so you end up with `Mods\FFRArchipelago\Scripts`.
3. Double-click `ff_resonance.apworld` or copy it into Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder.
4. Generate a seed, open the **Final Fantasy Resonance Client** from the Archipelago Launcher, connect, and choose **New Game (Archipelago)** in game.

If you tested an earlier build, delete `ap_in.txt`, `ap_out.txt`, and `ap_state.txt` from the mod folder before starting a new seed.

Disable `FFRRandomizer` if you have it installed, since it edits the same tables. If you use `FFRS_QoL`, leave its encounter rate at `1` and turn off its EXP, gil, MRP, and encounter overrides. Other mods are untested.

## Not done yet

- Quests, NPCs, and some pickup spots that give key items or visions in the base game are not locations yet. They still grant their normal rewards, so you can end up with a key item or vision twice.
- The Research Center Ruins' key cards and the Zoldaad Throne Key are not locations yet.
- Espers are still obtained the vanilla way, except Siren, which you start with.
- The Chamber of Arms and the Arena are untouched.
- Boss logic is still new. If logic sends you to a boss you cannot beat, or locks out one you beat easily, report it with your party members, levels, visions, Magicite, and gear.
- Some of the places behind bosses were worked out from map positions. If logic puts something behind the wrong boss, or something you can reach is out of logic, please report it.

## Reporting

Use the feedback template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md). For a crash or a missed check, include `ue4ss\UE4SS.log` and, after a crash, the newest folder in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FFRS\Saved\Crashes\`.
