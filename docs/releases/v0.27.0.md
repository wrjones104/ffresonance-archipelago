# v0.27.0 (apworld 0.27.0, mod 0.58.0): alpha pre-test

This is the first public alpha of the Final Fantasy Resonance randomizer for [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg/). Expect bugs and unfinished pieces. If you try a seed, please report how it went using the feedback template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md), which also covers what to look out for.

## Downloads

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `FFRArchipelago.zip` | UE4SS Lua game mod (v0.58.0) |
| `ff_resonance.apworld` | Archipelago world and Final Fantasy Resonance Client (v0.27.0) |
| `Final Fantasy Resonance.yaml` | Template player YAML with default options |

Make sure the mod and the apworld come from the same release.

## What is in this build

### Open-world start

Pick **New Game (Archipelago)** on the title screen to skip the story and drop onto your starting continent with your starting party, in the airship or on foot. Normal **New Game** and your existing vanilla saves stay untouched; the mod does nothing while a vanilla save is loaded.

### Locations and items

A default seed has 466 locations:

| Kind | Count |
|---|---|
| Treasure chests | 206 |
| World map and dungeon shinies | 92 |
| Tablet Shard shinies | 99 |
| Sanctum of Light crystals | 16 |
| Boss fights (21 story bosses, 15 cave masters, 4 others) | 40 |
| Airship radar treasures | 13 |

Story bosses hold Crystal Fragments for the `final_boss` goal. Aside from Sephiroth, the remaining bosses are priority locations by default.

Along with regular equipment, consumables, gil, and magicite, the item pool can contain:
- 9 continent Unlocks
- 8 party members (all except your starting party), joining at your party's average level
- 26 visions
- 29 key items (keys, key cards, quest items, Colosseum proofs)
- The Airship and Chocobo, if you do not start with them
- 3 Progressive Radars for airship radar treasures
- 99 Tablet Shards

Continents stay sealed until you receive their Unlock item, which blocks airship landings, both bridges, and ferry routes to them. Without the Airship, you travel on foot and by ferry, so Mysidia, Gronoa, Pharm, and the Crystal Tower are unreachable until you get it.

### Goals and options

Two goals are supported:
- `final_boss` (default): the top of the Crystal Tower stays sealed until you collect 6 party members, 12 visions, and 14 Crystal Fragments, then win the final battle.
- `tablet_shards`: receive 50 Tablet Shards.

YAML options cover the goal and its required counts, starting continent, starting party (1 to 4 of the 8 members by name), starting with the Airship or Chocobo, radar treasures on or off, priority bosses, filler-only regions, and whether party members, visions, and key items are shuffled. The template YAML documents each option.

### Logic and in-game features

Locations enter logic once you can reach them, and boss fights also check whether your party can win. Bosses require a party size and continent count based on their tier, a healer from the middle tiers up, and for many bosses a way to hit their weakness or survive their worst attack. Character skills count from the level they are learned, vision skills count by how many continents you have opened, and gear counts only when a reachable shop sells it. [Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases) shows what is in logic while you play.

In game:
- Enemies and bosses scale to your party level, with bosses scaling a bit higher.
- At the bottom of **Options > Gameplay**, you can change scaling strength (Casual, Normal, Expert), EXP, gil, and MRP rates, random encounters, and the encounter rate at any time.
- A feed at the bottom right shows items as they arrive, with progress counts on goal items (such as `Crystal Fragment (3/14)`).
- Chests holding another player's item tell you whose it is and what it is.
- The client links the save created by **New Game (Archipelago)** to your slot. Use `/bind` to link another save, or `/resync` to resend any missed checks.

## Requirements and installation

You need *Final Fantasy Resonance* on Steam (Windows), a UE4SS build for Unreal Engine 5.6 (`v3.0.1 Beta` or newer), and Archipelago 0.6.7 or newer. See the [README](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago#installation) for full details, or follow the quick steps below:

1. Install UE4SS into `FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE\FFRS\Binaries\Win64\`.
2. Extract `FFRArchipelago.zip` into `...\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\` so you end up with `Mods\FFRArchipelago\Scripts`.
3. Double-click `ff_resonance.apworld` or copy it into Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder.
4. Generate a seed, open the **Final Fantasy Resonance Client** from the Archipelago Launcher, connect, and choose **New Game (Archipelago)** in game.

If you tested an earlier build, delete `ap_in.txt`, `ap_out.txt`, and `ap_state.txt` from the mod folder before starting a new seed.

Disable `FFRRandomizer` if you have it installed, since it edits the same tables. If you use `FFRS_QoL`, leave its encounter rate at `1` and turn off its EXP, gil, MRP, and encounter overrides. Other mods are untested.

## Not done yet

- Quests, NPCs, and some pickup spots that give key items or visions in the base game are not locations yet. They still grant their normal rewards, so you can end up with a key item or vision twice.
- Espers are still obtained the vanilla way, except Siren, which you start with.
- The Chamber of Arms and the Arena are untouched.
- Boss logic is new. If logic sends you to a boss you cannot beat, or locks out one you beat easily, report it with your party members, levels, visions, and gear.

## Reporting

Use the feedback template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md). For a crash or a missed check, include `ue4ss\UE4SS.log` and, after a crash, the newest folder in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FFRS\Saved\Crashes\`.
