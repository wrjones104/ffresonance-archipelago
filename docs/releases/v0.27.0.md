# v0.27.0 (apworld 0.27.0, mod 0.58.0): alpha pre-test

This is the first public build of the Final Fantasy Resonance randomizer for [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg/). It is an alpha for testers: expect bugs and rough edges, and please report what you find. The [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md) says what to look out for and has a feedback template.

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `FFRArchipelago.zip` | The game mod (UE4SS Lua), version 0.58.0 |
| `ff_resonance.apworld` | The Archipelago world and the Final Fantasy Resonance Client, version 0.27.0 |
| `Final Fantasy Resonance.yaml` | A template player yaml with every option at its default |

Always install the mod and the apworld from the same release.

## What it does

**An open world from the start.** Pick **New Game (Archipelago)** on the title screen. The mod skips the story and starts you on your starting continent with your starting party, in the airship or on foot. Plain **New Game** and your other saves stay vanilla: the mod does nothing while one of them is loaded.

**Locations.** A default seed has 466:

| Kind | Count |
|---|---|
| Treasure chests | 206 |
| Shinies on the world map and in dungeons | 92 |
| Tablet Shard shinies | 99 |
| Sanctum of Light crystals | 16 |
| Boss fights: 21 story bosses, 15 cave masters, 4 others | 40 |
| Airship radar treasures | 13 |

Each story boss holds a Crystal Fragment for the final boss goal. The other bosses, except Sephiroth, are priority locations by default, so their items are worth the fight.

**Items.** Beside the game's own equipment, consumables, gil and magicite, the pool can hold:
- 9 continent Unlocks
- the 8 party members (all but your starting party), who join at your party's average level
- the 26 visions
- 29 key items (keys, key cards, quest items, Colosseum proofs)
- the Airship and the Chocobo, if you do not start with them
- 3 Progressive Radars for the radar treasures
- 99 Tablet Shards

**Continents and travel.** Each continent opens when you receive its Unlock. Until then the airship cannot land there, and the two bridges and the ferry will not take you there. Without the Airship you travel on foot and by ferry, and Mysidia, Gronoa, Pharm and the Crystal Tower wait for it.

**Goals.**
- `final_boss` (default): the way to the top of the Crystal Tower stays sealed until you have enough party members (6), visions (12) and Crystal Fragments (14). Then win the last battle.
- `tablet_shards`: receive a number of Tablet Shards (50 by default).

**Logic.** A location is in logic once you can reach it and, for a boss, once your party can win. A boss needs a party size and continent count by its tier, a healer from the middle tiers up, and for many bosses its weakness or a way through its worst attack. A character's skill counts from the level they learn it. A vision's skills count by how many continents you have opened. Gear counts only when a shop you can reach sells it. [Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases) shows what is in logic as you play.

**In the game.**
- Enemies and bosses scale to your party's level, with bosses a little stronger.
- At the bottom of **Options > Gameplay** you can change the scaling strength (Casual, Normal, Expert), EXP, gil and MRP rates, random encounters and the encounter rate at any time.
- A feed at the bottom right of the screen shows each item as it arrives. Goal items show a count, such as "Crystal Fragment (3/14)".
- A chest that holds another player's item tells you whose it is and what it is.
- The client links the save made by **New Game (Archipelago)** to your slot. `/bind` links another save, and `/resync` sends any checks the client may have missed.

**Options.** Goal and its counts, starting continent, starting party (1 to 4 of the 8 members, by name), starting with the Airship or the Chocobo, radar treasures on or off, priority bosses, regions with your own filler only, and whether party members, visions and key items are shuffled. The template yaml explains each one.

## Requirements and install

You need *Final Fantasy Resonance* on Steam (Windows), a UE4SS build for Unreal Engine 5.6 (`v3.0.1 Beta` or newer), and Archipelago 0.6.7 or newer. The [README](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago#installation) has the steps:

1. Install UE4SS into `FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE\FFRS\Binaries\Win64\`.
2. Extract `FFRArchipelago.zip` into `...\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\`, so you have `Mods\FFRArchipelago\Scripts`.
3. Double-click `ff_resonance.apworld`, or copy it into Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder.
4. Generate a seed, open the **Final Fantasy Resonance Client** from the Archipelago Launcher, connect, then pick **New Game (Archipelago)** in the game.

If you tested an earlier build, delete `ap_in.txt`, `ap_out.txt` and `ap_state.txt` from the mod's folder before you start a new seed.

Turn off `FFRRandomizer` if you have it: it changes the same tables. If you use `FFRS_QoL`, leave its encounter rate at 1 and turn off its EXP, gil, MRP and encounter overrides. Other mods are untested.

## Not done yet

- Quests, NPCs and some pick-up spots that give key items or visions in the base game are not locations yet. They still give their normal reward, so you can get a key item or vision twice.
- Espers are given as in the base game, except Siren, which you have from the start.
- The Chamber of Arms and the Arena are untouched.
- Boss logic is new. If logic sent you to a boss you could not beat, or kept you away from one you beat easily, please tell us your party, levels, visions and gear.

## Reporting

Use the feedback template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md). For a crash or a missed check, send `ue4ss\UE4SS.log` and, after a crash, the newest folder in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FFRS\Saved\Crashes\`.

Most of this project's code and docs, these notes included, were written by an AI (Claude) and tested by people. [DISCLOSURE.md](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/DISCLOSURE.md) has the details.
