# v0.30.0 (apworld 0.30.0, mod 0.61.0): public playtest, third build

The third public playtest build of the Final Fantasy Resonance randomizer for [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg/). Report how your seed went with the feedback template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md).

## Downloads

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `FFRArchipelago.zip` | UE4SS Lua game mod (v0.61.0, build `0e1022be`) |
| `ff_resonance.apworld` | Archipelago world and Final Fantasy Resonance Client (v0.30.0, build `fbcc579c`) |
| `Final Fantasy Resonance.yaml` | Template player YAML with default options |

Use the mod and the apworld from the same release. The mod logs `version 0.61.0 (build 0e1022be)` to `UE4SS.log` on load, and the client prints its own build when it starts. Replace the whole `FFRArchipelago` folder (this build has new script files) and generate a new seed: seeds from v0.29.0 do not know the new locations. Install steps are in the [README](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago#installation).

## Please test

Turn on `include_side_quests` (off by default) to cover the quest items. For each, note whether it worked, and send `UE4SS.log` if it did not.

- **The Archipelago page.** Open it from the in-game main menu. Check that the **Settings** tab changes EXP, gil, MRP, encounters, encounter rate and scaling, and that the **Seed** tab shows your goal, checks and continents, and scrolls with a gamepad.
- **Bosses.** Every boss, story bosses included, now gives an Archipelago item and a Crystal Fragment. Check that:
  - each win shows a pop-up with the item;
  - the feed says `Received Crystal Fragment (N/M)`;
  - loading a save with bosses beaten before this build sends their fragments.
- **Boss options.** Generate once with `priority_story_bosses: true` (new, off by default) and once with `priority_bosses` (now "Priority Cave Masters"), and check where progression lands.
- **NPC gifts.** 8 one-time gifts are locations, such as the Colosseum old man (Lunar Sword and Brigandine) and the Magi-tenna NPC in Gaberada (with radar treasures on). Check that:
  - the pop-up names the Archipelago item, not a Potion;
  - a chest opened near a gift NPC you have not talked to yet shows the chest's own item.
- **Research Center Ruins key cards.** The three key card spots are locations, and the doors open with the cards Archipelago sends.
- **Side quests:**
  - **Pop-ups:** the reward pop-up names the Archipelago item, including Token Legacy, Mog Tag and The Call from Beyond.
  - **Second rewards:** a quest that pays a second item, a vision or gil gives a second location. Its pop-up names both items, and the game gives none of them itself.
  - **Quests screen:** the Rewards list names what each quest holds.
  - **Verun's Mechabo quests:** "Arise, Mechabo!", "Mechabo Evolved" and "Mechabo Unleashed" can be done in order, with Lid learning Mechabo Tackle at the second.
- **Quest logic past Liquid Thunder.** On foot in Dirnado without the Airship, Lyrical Riddles and Elven Maven should be out of logic until Liquid Thunder is.
- **Item colours.** Item names are coloured by Archipelago class (light blue filler, dark blue useful, purple progression, red trap) in three places: the item feed, the chest pop-up and the Quests screen. Items the server sends (your starting continent's unlock, `!getitem`) should show their real class.

## Known issues

- **A pop-up can show the game's own item.** The check is still sent; only the pop-up text is wrong. Two known cases:
  - when two checks come at once (a chest right after a boss win), the second one's pop-up;
  - Iron Giant's win pop-up, in one test.
- **Two NPC gifts are not locations:** the Ether NPC in the Commercial District and the Mist Potion NPC in Grandshelt Castle. They were not found at the open world start. They give their normal items if you find them.
- **Character quests' stat bonuses** (such as "Attribute Bonus: Fina") are not items. The quest gives them as in the base game.
- **ModSettings crash.** The `ModSettings` UE4SS Lua mod (`Mods\ModSettings`, from FFR Modding Tools) can crash the game a second or two after a map change, with or without this mod. To avoid it, do one of these:
  - turn off **Hint on map load** in the ModSettings panel (F10);
  - set `ModSettings : 0` in `Mods\mods.txt`. This turns off only its Lua key binding; the ModSettings Blueprint mod still works.
- **Mt. Thunderclap's shortcut ladder has no mesh.** Examine at the top of the cliff lets it down and you can climb it, but it is invisible.
- **Not done yet:** espers other than Siren come the vanilla way, and the Chamber of Arms and the Arena are untouched.
