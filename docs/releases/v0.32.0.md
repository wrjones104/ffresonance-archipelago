# v0.32.0 (apworld 0.32.0, mod 0.63.0): public playtest, fifth build

## GitHub Release Notes

The big change this time is the start. New Game (Archipelago) no longer plays the prologue and reloads. You start on foot next to your starting continent's main town, right away, and it works with autosave off too. There's also a bigger item pool, Aileen's forge as an optional set of locations, a pile of logic and key item fixes, and fast travel now only goes to places you've actually walked into. I also spent a couple of rounds on a stutter some of you reported. Please send feedback with the template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md).

### Install and upgrade

- Use the mod and the apworld from the same release. The mod logs `version 0.63.0 (build 2a1d876c)` to `UE4SS.log` when it loads, and the client prints `world version 0.32.0 (build e0989726)` when it starts.
- Replace the whole `FFRArchipelago` folder in `ue4ss\Mods`, and put the new apworld in `custom_worlds`. Close the Launcher and every client window first.
- Generate a new seed. The pool, the logic and the locations changed, and the template yaml now asks for world 0.32.0.
- Start the client and connect it before you pick **New Game (Archipelago)** on the title screen. Without it the entry says "connect the client" and opens Load Game instead.
- Install steps are in the [README](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago#installation).

### What's changed since v0.31.0

**A new start.** **New Game (Archipelago)** now builds the seed's game directly. There's no opening, no Archwitch Fina section, and no reload into the open world. You land on foot next to your starting continent's main town with your starting party at level 1. If the seed starts with the airship, it's parked right there next to you. This used to depend on the game's first autosave, so with autosave off the seed played the prologue and chapter 1 with Rain at level 55. That's gone.

- You can't walk until the mod has put you at the town (a second or two), so you can't wander off mid-move and freeze.
- The client now writes a heartbeat file while it's connected. With no live client, the title entry reads **New Game (Archipelago: connect the client)** and opens Load Game with a notice, so you can't start a seed by accident on an old seed's settings.
- Active Time Chats from the skipped story don't pop up at the start.

**Item pool.**

- 2 Ribbons and 13 accessories that used to be enemy-only are in the pool now.
- New `extra_magicite` option (0 to 30, default 9): Magicite added on top of what the chests, shinies and bosses hold, split between Shards, Magicite and Crysts.
- New `early_healing_vision` option (on by default, needs `shuffle_visions`): one vision that can cast Cure or Esuna goes early in your own world.
- The Proofs of Victory are out of the pool. The Colosseum gives each one at its rank clear anyway.

**Aileen's forge.** New `include_aileens_forge` option (off by default). The forge on the airship deck becomes eight locations: your first forged weapon, your second, and so on. It opens after Forger of Wills, each weapon costs one Adamantite, and eight Adamantite go in the pool. You still get the weapon you pick.

**Key items.**

- With `shuffle_key_items` on, the squad leader in Zoldaad Castle no longer hands you the Throne Room Key, and the man by Sian's inn no longer gives the Cabin Key.
- With it off, the Dungeon Key is given when you walk into Zoldaad Dungeon (its soldier never shows up in an open world seed), and the locations behind each key are in logic behind the game's own giver.

**Logic fixes.**

- The Abandoned Cabin's chest needs the Cabin Key, and two Zoldaad Castle chests need the Throne Room Key.
- Token Legacy and Errand's End need the Throne Room Key. Beyond the Blue needs a party of 4.
- Two shinies on Zoldaad's south-west tip and the Dirnado Far-SW Headland shiny need the airship.

**Fast travel.**

- Fast travel only goes to places you've walked into. A quest scene that carries you somewhere (The Tide from the Sky drops you in Grandport, the Ayaka quest runs through Zoldaad) doesn't count until you walk in yourself. Existing saves keep the places they already had.
- Places on locked continents aren't fast travel targets.
- The airship counts as visited when the seed starts with it, so it isn't grey on the Map screen.

**Things that stayed stuck after a fast travel out are fixed.** Fast travelling out of a dungeon used to leave things the way you left them, because in the base game you always walk out the way that resets them.

- Tower of Mysidia: taking the lift up and fast travelling out left the lift at the top for good, so you could never get back up.
- The lifts in the Wind Shrine, Water Shrine, Gronoa Shrine and Sanctissimus Shrine go back to where they were on your first visit.
- The rafts in Cave of Waters and the Water Shrine too.
- Kolobos Ruins: the floor that falls under you is put back, as Rain's "elbow grease" scene does, so the way in isn't blocked.

**The airship.** On a seed where the airship is an item, it now shows up where you started the seed, not wherever the game felt like putting it (one came up near Zoldaad on a Dirnado start).

**Other fixes.**

- The first vision Moogle you talk to no longer plays Grandport's Moogle scene and leaves you out of bounds. Grandport Harbor has its vision Moogle now, so a Lanzelt start can upgrade visions.
- Reloading the Archipelago game's save no longer gives you every early item a second time.
- The two names in a two-reward pop-up don't touch anymore.
- The radar goes quiet once everything it can find is dug, like in the base game, until your next Progressive Radar.
- Lid's "we already found Mysidia" line on the airship is gone.
- The Seed tab on the Archipelago page says `Start: on foot on <continent>, next to your airship`.

**The stutter.** A few of you felt a steady stutter, like a heartbeat. The mod's loop runs twice a second, and some of its steps did a lot of work at once. I split the slow steps across ticks and took out the worst ones: a full search of the talk event table every second for two rows, and a hook that retried every second until you opened the Quests screen. On my desktop the slow ticks went from 13 to 60 every 2 minutes down to about 5. The log now gets `perf:` lines every 2 minutes, and `ffrap_perf` in the console writes them right away.

### Please test

- **The stutter, especially on slower PCs and laptops.** Play for 10 minutes or so, towns included, and send the `perf:` lines from `UE4SS.log`. If you feel a stutter, run `ffrap_perf` right then and tell me.
- **The new start.** Start a fresh seed with autosave on and off. You should land next to your continent's town with no opening, and with the airship next to you if the seed starts with it. Also try the title with the client closed.
- **Fast travel.** A place a scene took you to shouldn't be a fast travel target until you walk in. Tell me if the Map screen still shows or travels to such a place.
- **Lifts and rafts.** In the shrines above, ride a lift or raft, fast travel out, and come back in by the door. Everything should be where it was on your first visit.
- **Story boss pop-ups.** When you beat a story boss, what did the item pop-up say? On my last run the mod didn't write into it, though the item still came in the feed.
- **`include_aileens_forge`**, if you turn it on: each forged weapon should send one check, in order.
- **`shuffle_key_items: false`**: walk into Zoldaad Dungeon, and the Dungeon Key should be in your bag with the cells open.

### Known issues

- A story boss's item pop-up can show the game's stand-in item instead of the Archipelago item. The check still goes out and the item comes in the feed.
- The radar: Lid can say it stopped finding treasure while some are still left. A Progressive Radar received while flying turns the radar on, but its treasures only dig after a map change.
- The "connect the client" label on the title only updates when the title screen is made again (quit to the title once).
- Mobliz Shipyard's last lift still doesn't come up, so two chests and two shards there stay out of reach.
- When two checks come at once (a chest right after a boss win), the second pop-up can show the game's own item. The check is still sent.
- Character quests' stat bonuses (like "Attribute Bonus: Fina") aren't items. The quest gives them as in the base game.
- The `ModSettings` Lua mod (from FFR Modding Tools) can crash the game a second or two after a map change, with or without this mod. To avoid it, turn off **Hint on map load** in its panel (F10), or set `ModSettings : 0` in `Mods\mods.txt`.
- Mt. Thunderclap's shortcut ladder is invisible. You can still let it down from the top and climb it.
- Not done yet: espers other than Siren come the vanilla way, and the Chamber of Arms and the Arena are untouched.
- If you crash, send `Mods/FFRArchipelago/ap_trace.txt`, the crash folder in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FFRS\Saved\Crashes`, and `UE4SS.log` before you start the game again.

Most of this project's code and docs were written with Claude and tested by hand; see [DISCLOSURE.md](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/DISCLOSURE.md) for details.
