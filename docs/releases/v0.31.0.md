# v0.31.0 (apworld 0.31.0, mod 0.62.0): public playtest, fourth build

## GitHub Release Notes

This one is mostly about enemy scaling. After a lot of AP runs I played some vanilla, and the enemies there are much softer than our Normal. Hallway fights in an AP run were long, and later bosses were too hard. So I've toned scaling down across the board. There are a few bug fixes too. Please send feedback with the template in the [Playtester guide](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/docs/playtester_guide.md).

### Downloads

### Install and upgrade

- Use the mod and the apworld from the same release. The mod logs `version 0.62.0 (build 3245e5b1)` to `UE4SS.log` when it loads, and the client prints `world version 0.31.0 (build 7c8d8ec3)` when it starts.
- Replace the whole `FFRArchipelago` folder in `ue4ss\Mods`, and put the new apworld in `custom_worlds`. Close the Launcher and every client window first.
- Generate a new seed. Seeds from v0.30.0 have a gift location this build no longer has, and the template yaml now asks for world 0.31.0.
- Install steps are in the [README](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago#installation).

### What's changed since v0.30.0

**Scaling is softer.** Every **AP: Scaling strength** moved down a step:

| Strength | Enemy level | Enemy HP | Enemy Attack | Boss level | Boss power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Casual | 80% to 88% of yours | 60% to 75% | 75% to 90% | 95% to 100% | 90% to 100% |
| Normal | 85% to 92% | 70% to 85% | 85% to 100% | 100% to 103% | 100% to 105% |
| Expert | 90% to 100% | 80% to 105% | 90% to 115% | 105% to 110% | 105% to 110% |

Each range ramps from party level 5 to 25.

- On Normal, enemies now sit a bit under your level. They lose more HP than Attack, so fights end sooner but can still hurt if you're careless.
- Enemies from areas ahead of you used to be 3 levels over the band's middle on Normal, which is where the spongy fights came from. Now they're 1 over.
- Bosses on Normal sit at about your level.
- Expert is roughly the old Normal with a bit less HP. If you want a real challenge, try Expert scaling with the game on Expert too.
- Above party level 30, enemy levels grow 3 levels for every 4 you gain. The game's own enemy stats grow much faster than the party's in the late game, so enemies held at your level kept pulling ahead. At level 60 on Normal, the final boss now meets you at 54 instead of 66.

**MRP per fight stays the same.** Normally, lower enemy levels would mean less MRP per battle. Now MRP is paid as if the enemies were at your level, so the vision grind gets shorter, not longer, now that fights end sooner. If you want even less grind, the **AP: MRP rate** slider is still there.

**Fixes:**

- Some chests read as opened as soon as you arrived on a map and got sent before you touched them. The mod now waits until the game's save says the chest was really opened.
- If you play the same save on two computers, chests you'd already opened on the other one get sent again with no pop-up, so they don't steal the next chest's pop-up anymore.
- The coloured item line no longer sticks around on a quest's later messages (the stat bonus and "You can now summon..." lines).
- Starting a plain **New Game**, or loading one of your own non-AP saves, no longer gets any of the seed's changes. A plain New Game used to start Rain at level 55. If you load the seed's save and then one of your own in the same session, the mod tells you to restart the game first.

**Gifts:**

- NPC gift locations are now named for where the NPC stands (like "Grandshelt - Colosseum - Upper East Terrace Gift") instead of the item they give in the base game.
- I removed the Zoldaad Dungeon gift because it can't be reached from the open world start.

### Please test

Scaling is the big one. Note your **AP: Scaling strength** and the game's own difficulty when you report.

- How long a normal fight takes on Normal, both in areas around your level and in areas ahead of you. They should be quick but not free.
- Whether MRP per fight feels the same as before, and whether the vision grind feels better.
- Bosses at party level 30 and up. Are they fair with what the seed has given you?
- Levels 40 to 60, and the final boss at about level 60. If it's too easy up there now, tell me. The softer strengths and the late-game slowdown stack, and I'd rather tune the slowdown than the strengths.
- Expert scaling with the game on Expert, if that's your thing. Hard but winnable is the goal.

Bug fixes to check:

- On arrival in a new map, look for `never seen closed` lines in `UE4SS.log`. If a chest from one of those lines looks open in the game and you can never open it, send me its key (like `chest:3100:3`).
- If you play one save on two computers, open a new chest on the second one and check that its pop-up shows its own item.
- Finish a quest with a stat bonus or an esper, like Nichol's Duty in Olderion. The coloured item line should only be on the first message.
- Start a plain **New Game** with the client connected. Rain should stay at level 1, and the log should say `Archipelago is paused`.

### Known issues

- When two checks come at once (a chest right after a boss win), the second pop-up can show the game's own item. The check is still sent.
- Three NPC gifts aren't locations: the Ether NPC in the Commercial District, the Mist Potion NPC in Grandshelt Castle, and the Zoldaad Dungeon NPC. They give their normal items if you find them.
- Character quests' stat bonuses (like "Attribute Bonus: Fina") aren't items. The quest gives them as in the base game.
- I've had one crash about 6 hours in, a few seconds after landing the airship, with FFR_MapMarkers and FFRBestiary on. It doesn't look like this mod's loop, but I'm not sure yet. If you crash, send `Mods/FFRArchipelago/ap_trace.txt`, the crash folder in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FFRS\Saved\Crashes`, and `UE4SS.log` before you start the game again.
- The `ModSettings` Lua mod (from FFR Modding Tools) can crash the game a second or two after a map change, with or without this mod. To avoid it, turn off **Hint on map load** in its panel (F10), or set `ModSettings : 0` in `Mods\mods.txt`.
- Mt. Thunderclap's shortcut ladder is invisible. You can still let it down from the top and climb it.
- Not done yet: espers other than Siren come the vanilla way, and the Chamber of Arms and the Arena are untouched.

Most of this project's code and docs were written with Claude and tested by hand; see [DISCLOSURE.md](https://github.com/wrjones104/ffresonance-archipelago/blob/main/DISCLOSURE.md) for details.
